Loop Line · one brief, three topologies

A chain goes forward.
A graph can go back.

The same seeded marketing agent — brief → research → plan → create → review — runs three ways. The chain dead-ends on a failed review. The while-loop retries but wipes its state. The graph rides one back-edge and ships. Only the topology changed.

Educational model. Every step runs locally in your browser against a deterministic, seeded pipeline — no LLM calls, nothing deployed, timings illustrative.
